Use notice

Personal Instinct Bridge is an individually configured installed utility. This website is documentation, not an offer of a hosted service or public access to private systems.

Authorized operation only

The utility must be operated only with permission from the relevant account holder. Message content is treated as data, not instructions. Outward actions require the configured authentication, exact recipient and content binding, approval, expiry, and replay checks. Uncertain execution must be reconciled instead of blindly retried.

Limits

Available source history can be incomplete. A service acceptance receipt is not proof of recipient delivery or reading. Media and call transcript coverage are not guaranteed. Third-party account rules, availability, authorization expiry, and sending limits continue to apply.
